- Equipment: Hario Switch.
- Dose: 20.0 g (0.71 oz)
- Water: 320 g (11.29 oz) — brew yield
- Ratio: 1 : 16
- Grind: 19 clicks on Timemore C2 Max.
- Water temperature: 94 °C (201 °F)
- Total brew time: 3:00-3:20

|   Time    | Instruction                                                                                                            | Pour | Total |
| :-------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ----- |
|   0:00    | Rinse filter thoroughly and preheat brewer.                                                                            | 0g   | 0g    |
| 0:00-0:45 | Bloom with **60 g**, swirl once at 0:10. Make sure to use enough agitation to fully wet the larger Pacamara particles. | 60g  | 60g   |
| 0:45-1:15 | Pour to **170 g** in slow concentric spirals.                                                                          | 110g | 170g  |
| 1:15-1:45 | Pour to **250 g**. Keep slurry height moderate, avoid aggressive center dumping.                                       | 80g  | 250g  |
| 1:45-2:05 | Pour to 320 g                                                                                                          | 70g  | 320g  |
|   2:05    | Gentle Swirl to flatten the bed.                                                                                       | 0g   | 320g  |
|   3:20    | Drawdown should finish
